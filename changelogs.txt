Build date: 17/11/2026
Sonara v2.1.0 — Sonara's own look. Installs over any BitChord PlusPC or Sonara build and keeps your library, downloads, settings and sign-in.
- Your Sonara Pass: a card with your name, pass number and a soundprint, made on the new welcome.
- Library is your profile: your Pass, then hours and minutes listened, plays, artists and your top artist.
- Settings opens on a grid of tiles; playlists and collaborative actions get tiles of their own.
- Switches glow Sonara red, the tab bar shows icons only, and Home greets you by the name on your Pass.
