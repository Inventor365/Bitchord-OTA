Sonara v2.0.0 — BitChord PlusPC is now Sonara. This update installs over BitChord in place and keeps your library, downloads, settings and sign-in.
- Home greets you by name and time of day, under the new Sonara mark.
- Search opens on a grid of moods and genres to browse.
- Settings leads with your account card, with a coloured icon for every category.
- An animated welcome, tinted Library folder cards, and cards that respond to touch on 60, 90 and 120 Hz screens.
- Collaborative playlists: any Google account can join; removing a song, leaving or deleting a playlist asks first.
- Everything from 1.10.x: reliable invite joins, full playlist conversion, title and cover editing, instant adds.
